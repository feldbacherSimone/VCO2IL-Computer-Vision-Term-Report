\chapter{Vision Based Sign Language Recognition}
This chapter provides an overview of current applications of gesture recognition in sign language translation. 
The following section will elaborate on the task definition of sign language translation as well as highlight some challanges and how they are adressed with different technologies. 
\section{Overview}
In the field of sign language translation there are two main goals. Sign production, which aims to generate visual output of a signing individual from an input phrase and sign interpretation, which refers to the conversion of a video feed into either written or aural output. The latter can be further divided into \textit{Continous Sign Langauge Regognition} (CLSR) and  \textit{Sign Language Translation} (SLT), where the former aims to identify individual signs in a video feed and the latter aims to translate a full sentence into written or spoken language.
