\begin{abstract}\noindent
This report presents two classical computer vision exercises and a short literature review on hand gesture and sign language recognition. The exercise part covers panorama stitching, feature matching, camera calibration, pose estimation, DCT-based image compression, and implicit neural representations. The research part summarizes the current state of hand gesture recognition and sign language translation, with attention to vision-based, sensor-based, and multimodal approaches, as well as current challenges in robustness, data availability, and real-time performance.
\end{abstract}
